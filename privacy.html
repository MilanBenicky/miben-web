Privacy policy

MiBeN Shopping List (Android). Last updated: 8 November 2026.

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is MiBeN s. r. o., Kvačalova 1167/31, 010 04 Žilina, Slovakia, company ID (IČO) 55611541 ("we"). Contact: miben.support@gmail.com.

2. In short

  • The app works without an account. Without an account, your lists, products and images stay on your phone and we do not receive them.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, your data is stored on our server so that it can be synced between your devices and shared with people you choose.
  • The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

3. Data on your device

The app stores your shopping lists, products, categories, shops, units, prices, notes and images you add in a database on your phone. This data is not sent anywhere unless you sign in and sync (section 4). If you uninstall the app, this local data is removed with it.

4. Account and sync (optional)

When you sign in with Google, we receive from Google:

  • a stable identifier of your Google account (not your password),
  • your e-mail address, if Google confirms it,
  • your display name, if Google provides it.

When you sync, the following is stored on our server and linked to your account:

  • your shopping lists and the items on them (names, quantities, units, prices, discounts, notes, bought status, and who added an item to a shared list),
  • products, categories, shops and units you created, and your personal settings (favourites, category choices, how often you added a product, last used units),
  • images you added to your own products (reduced in size by the app before upload),
  • your friend connections and the lists you share.

Sharing: when you share a list with a friend, that person can see the list, its items, the products and images used on it, and your e-mail address as a member of the list. Do not share a list with people you do not want to see this.

Your sign-in on a device is kept as an encrypted token on that device and expires if not used for 90 days.

5. Why we use the data and on what legal basis

  • To provide sync and sharing, which you request by signing in: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and stable, to prevent abuse and to fix errors, using the technical logs in section 6: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, for example in accounting, if paid features are offered: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

6. Technical logs

Our server is monitored with Microsoft Azure Monitor (Application Insights). It records technical information about requests to the server, such as time, requested address, response status, errors and IP address. We use it only to run, secure and troubleshoot the service. The content of your lists is not intended to be written to these logs. The logs are kept for a limited period only.

7. Who receives the data

  • Microsoft Azure hosts our server, database and image storage. It acts as our processor. Everything is stored in the Microsoft Azure region Poland Central (Warsaw, Poland), in the European Union.
  • Google provides sign-in with Google, and Google Play handles payments if you buy a subscription. We never see your card details.
  • Other users you share a list with, as described in section 4.

We do not use advertising networks and we do not pass your data to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

8. How long we keep the data

  • Account data stays while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from the server as described in Delete your account.
  • If paid features are offered and a subscription has ended, synced data is deleted 365 days after it ended.
  • Images that are no longer used by any product are deleted automatically.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing and to receive it in a portable form. Most of this you can do directly in the app. For anything else write to miben.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (dataprotection.gov.sk) or to the authority in your own country.

10. Children

The app is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Security

Data is sent to our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your device. No online service is perfectly secure, so we limit what we collect to what the app needs.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here with a new date. For significant changes we also inform you in the app.
