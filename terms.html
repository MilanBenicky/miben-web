Limits and fair use

MiBeN Shopping List (Android) by MiBeN s. r. o. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

The app is made for a household's everyday shopping. To keep it fast and fair for everyone, it has limits. Normal use never reaches them.

Limits in the app

WhatLimit
Shopping lists20
Items on one list200
Your own products (beyond the built-in catalog)5,000
Categories100
Shops200
Quantity units and price units100 each
Your own pictures5,000
Friends50
People on one shared list (including the owner)10

Names have a maximum length: product 40 characters, category 30, shop 20, unit 10, list name 40 and comment 250. Your own pictures are scaled to at most 720 pixels on the longer side and 50 kB. Data you already have is never deleted to meet a limit; the limit only stops you from adding more.

Fair use of the sync service

Synchronisation, sharing and the other online features run on our server, which we pay for. Per account and per day we allow about:

  • 5,000 changes sent to the server (a change is a created, edited or deleted item, list, product and so on),
  • 2,000 picture uploads and 512 MiB of uploaded pictures,
  • 200 sign-ins,
  • 10 devices signed in at the same time.

We also limit how many requests per minute one account can send. These numbers are far above normal use and may change; the page is updated when they do.

What happens when a limit is reached

  • The app keeps working on your phone: nothing is lost, you can still read and edit your lists.
  • Synchronisation pauses. A daily limit is lifted automatically by the next day's check (it runs at night, UTC).
  • If an account is blocked because of apparent abuse, it stays blocked until we review it. Write to miben.support@gmail.com and we will look at it.

One account, one person

  • Accounts are personal. Please do not share your account or its sign-in with other people; use friends and shared lists for that.
  • A subscription is for one person. The family plan, if offered, covers up to 5 people, each with their own account.
  • Do not use the service to send spam or to harm other users or the service.

Your data after a subscription ends

See the privacy policy: it says how long synced data is kept after a subscription ends and how to delete your account (delete account).
