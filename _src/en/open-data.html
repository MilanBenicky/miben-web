MiBeN Shopping List (Android) by MiBeN s. r. o. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

The app suggests a category while you type a product name, offline, from a table that is built partly from Open Food Facts.

Open Food Facts

Contains data from Open Food Facts, © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Individual contents of the database are available under the Database Contents License. We do not use product photos.

Our table

The table maps brands and words from product names to one of the 15 categories of the app. It is a derived database and we release it under the same licence, ODbL 1.0. It also contains brand lists we wrote ourselves and the names of the built-in catalog of the app.

  • brands.tsv – readable form (about 3 MB, tab-separated: market, key, category, confidence).
  • brands.bin – the compact file that is packed into the app (about 2.3 MB).
