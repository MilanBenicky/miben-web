MiBeN Shopping List

A shopping list app for Android. It also works offline and can share lists with friends.

What the app does

  • Create several lists and add products from a built-in catalog or your own.
  • Group products by category and by shop, with units, prices and notes.
  • Works without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and is used to sync your lists between your devices and to share lists with friends.
  • No advertising and no tracking in the app.
