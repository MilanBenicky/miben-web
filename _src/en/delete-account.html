Delete your account

MiBeN Shopping List (Android) by MiBeN s. r. o. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

You can delete your account and the data we hold about it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → My account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This also signs you out. Your lists on the phone stay on the phone until you remove them or uninstall the app.

If you cannot use the app

Write to miben.support@gmail.com from the e-mail address of the Google account you used, and ask us to delete your account. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it as soon as we can and at the latest within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your e-mail address.
  • Your lists and the items on them, your products, categories, shops, units and personal settings, and the images you uploaded.
  • Your friend connections and your membership in shared lists.
  • Your sign-in sessions.

What happens to shared lists

  • If a list you share has other members, it is handed over to the member who has been in it the longest and stays, with all its items, for the remaining members.
  • Products, shops, units and images that other people's lists still use are kept for them, but they no longer carry your name or any other personal data.
  • A list with no other members is deleted.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. If you have one, cancel it first in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

What we keep

We keep two technical records: an internal account identifier with the dates the account was created and deleted, and your Google sign-in identifier. They contain no lists, no images and no e-mail address. We keep them for as long as the service runs, so that an old copy of the app cannot sign in to the deleted account and so that abuse blocks stay in place. Items you added to lists that now belong to other members stay in those lists, marked only with this internal identifier. Database backups and deleted image files are kept by our hosting provider for 7 days and technical logs for up to 90 days; then they are deleted automatically (see the privacy policy).
